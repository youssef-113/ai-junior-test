# NovaBite Opening Hours

## Regular Hours (All Branches)

**Sunday - Thursday**
- Opening: 11:00 AM
- Last Order (Kitchen): 10:00 PM
- Closing: 11:00 PM

**Friday - Saturday**
- Opening: 12:00 PM (Noon)
- Last Order (Kitchen): 11:00 PM
- Closing: 12:00 AM (Midnight)

## Holiday Hours

**Eid Al-Fitr**
- Day 1-3: 2:00 PM - 10:00 PM

**Eid Al-Adha**
- Day 1-4: 2:00 PM - 10:00 PM

**Ramadan Hours**
- Iftar Service: Sunset - 11:00 PM
- Suhoor Service: 9:00 PM - 3:00 AM (Ramadan nights only)

**New Year's Eve**
- December 31: 11:00 AM - 2:00 AM

**Other Public Holidays**
- Regular weekend hours apply

## Reservation Times

Lunch service: 11:00 AM - 4:00 PM
Dinner service: 5:00 PM - 11:00 PM

Popular times that fill quickly:
- Friday dinner (7:00 PM - 9:00 PM)
- Saturday lunch (1:00 PM - 3:00 PM)
- Weekend brunch (11:00 AM - 1:00 PM)

## Best Times to Visit

**Quietest Times:**
- Sunday - Tuesday: 2:00 PM - 5:00 PM
- Weekday lunch (before 1:00 PM)

**Busiest Times:**
- Friday 7:00 PM - 10:00 PM
- Saturday 12:00 PM - 4:00 PM
- Holiday evenings

## Kitchen Closing Times

Last food orders accepted 1 hour before closing.
Drinks and desserts available until closing.

## Delivery Hours

Delivery available during all opening hours.
Last delivery order: 30 minutes before kitchen closing.
