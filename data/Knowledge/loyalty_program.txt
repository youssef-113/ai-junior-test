# NovaBite Loyalty Program

## Program Overview

Join our NovaBite Loyalty Program and earn points with every visit! Membership is free.

## How It Works

- Earn 1 point for every 1 EGP spent
- Redeem points for discounts and rewards
- 100 points = 50 EGP discount
- Minimum redemption: 100 points

## Membership Tiers

### Base Tier (0-299 points)
- Free drink with every meal
- Birthday dessert
- Member-exclusive offers

### Pro Tier (300-749 points)
- 15% discount on all food orders
- Free dessert monthly
- Priority booking for reservations
- All Base tier benefits

### Premium Tier (750+ points)
- 20% discount on all food orders
- Priority reservations (skip the wait)
- Free birthday meal (main + dessert)
- Exclusive event invitations
- Complimentary welcome drink
- All Pro tier benefits

## Redeeming Points

Points can be redeemed during checkout:
1. Inform your server you want to redeem points
2. Provide your User ID
3. Choose redemption amount (multiples of 100 points)
4. Discount applied immediately

## Special Events

Members receive special perks for:
- **Birthday:** Complimentary meal and dessert
- **Anniversary:** Free bottle of wine with dinner
- **Holidays:** Festive menu specials
- **Eid:** Traditional Eid menu with complimentary dessert

## Program Rules

- Points expire after 12 months of inactivity
- Cannot combine loyalty discount with other promotions
- Points are non-transferable
- Lost cards can be replaced (points remain)

## How to Join

Ask your server for a loyalty card or sign up online at novabite.com/loyalty
Your User ID will be provided upon registration (format: USRXXXX)
