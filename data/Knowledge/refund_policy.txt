# NovaBite Refund & Cancellation Policy

## Table Reservation Cancellations

**Standard Cancellations:**
- Cancel up to 2 hours before reservation: Full refund (if deposit paid)
- Cancel within 2 hours: No refund of deposit
- No-show without cancellation: No refund, loyalty points deducted

**Large Group Bookings (8+ guests):**
- Cancel up to 24 hours before: Full refund
- Cancel within 24 hours: 50% deposit retained
- No-show: Full deposit forfeited

**Special Events / Holidays:**
- Cancel up to 48 hours before: Full refund
- Cancel within 48 hours: Deposit non-refundable

## Food Quality Issues

If you are unsatisfied with your meal:
- Inform server immediately
- We will replace the dish or offer an alternative
- Refund available if replacement not acceptable

**Conditions:**
- Must report issue before consuming majority of dish
- Applies to food quality, incorrect orders, or allergens not disclosed
- Does not apply to personal taste preferences

## Refund Methods

- Card payments: Refunded to original card (3-5 business days)
- Cash payments: Immediate cash refund or store credit
- Loyalty points: Restored immediately upon approval

## Event Deposits

Private events and catering require deposits:
- Small events (8-20 guests): 25% deposit
- Medium events (21-50 guests): 50% deposit
- Large events (51+ guests): 50% deposit

**Cancellation terms vary by event size and notice given.**

## Force Majeure

In case of extreme weather, government restrictions, or other emergencies:
- Deposits can be transferred to future date
- Full refunds available if rescheduling not possible
- 48 hours notice required for weather-related cancellations

## How to Cancel or Modify

- Phone: Call the branch directly
- Online: Use confirmation link in booking email
- Walk-in: Speak with host (not recommended for same-day cancellations)

## Contact for Refunds

For refund inquiries, contact:
- Branch manager on duty
- Customer service: care@novabite.com

All refund requests reviewed within 24 hours.
