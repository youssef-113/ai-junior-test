# NovaBite Allergen Information Guide

## Common Allergens Present in Our Kitchen

We take food allergies seriously. Please inform your server of any allergies when ordering.

### Allergen Categories

**Gluten**
- Present in: Pasta, bread, breadcrumbs, flour, soy sauce
- Our pasta contains wheat. Gluten-free pasta available on request (limited availability).
- Cross-contamination may occur in kitchen.

**Dairy**
- Present in: Cheese, cream, butter, milk, yogurt, ice cream
- Vegan cheese available for some dishes.

**Eggs**
- Present in: Pasta, breading, mayonnaise, desserts
- Can often be substituted or omitted.

**Seafood / Fish**
- Present in: Calamari, sea bass, salmon, anchovies, tuna
- Separate preparation area available.

**Shellfish**
- Present in: Shrimp, clams, mussels
- Not suitable for guests with shellfish allergies.

**Peanuts**
- Present in: Peanut sauce, some Asian dishes
- Can be omitted from most dishes.

**Tree Nuts**
- Present in: Pesto (cashews), Nutella (hazelnuts), desserts
- Some dishes can be prepared without nuts.

**Soy**
- Present in: Soy sauce, Beyond Burger patties, tofu dishes
- Coconut aminos available as substitute.

**Sesame**
- Present in: Hummus, tahini, some salads
- Can often be omitted.

Our chefs can accommodate most dietary requirements with advance notice:
- Vegan
- Vegetarian
- Gluten-free (limited options)
- Dairy-free
- Nut-free

Please inform your server of any allergies or dietary restrictions.
